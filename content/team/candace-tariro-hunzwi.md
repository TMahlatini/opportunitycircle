Candace Tariro Hunzwi is a Zimbabwean computer scientist pursuing a Master’s in Engineering Artificial Intelligence at Carnegie Mellon University. She holds a BSc in Computer Science from Ashesi University and has worked on African language technologies, sign language research, and AI-powered tools. She is passionate about building practical, accessible technology that helps people turn their skills into meaningful opportunities.
