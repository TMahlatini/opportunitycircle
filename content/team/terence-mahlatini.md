Terence Mahlatini is a Software Engineer at Multicoreware Inc., specializing in machine learning systems and related compiler technology. Alongside his engineering career, he is deeply passionate about economic, technological, and human capital development in Zimbabwe. His experiences with Zimbabwean NGOs, including Uncommon and EdMatters, fuel his vision for scalable, sustainable solutions that empower the country's youth. Terence holds a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Whitman College.
