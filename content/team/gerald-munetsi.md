Gerald Munetsi is a Zimbabwean Mastercard Foundation Scholar who holds an MPhil in Population Health Sciences from the University of Cambridge. He is a researcher and data scientist whose work focuses on the intersection of infectious disease modelling and deep learning approaches for global health applications. Beyond his technical work, Gerald has led several youth development and outreach initiatives and engaged stakeholders to support impactful social programmes.
