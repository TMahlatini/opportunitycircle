The 2026 Innovation Challenge convened by Education Matters and Kutunga brings teams together to work on one problem: how Zimbabweans can turn their education, skills and experience into sustainable income. It is about using technology, innovation and collaboration to build an income that lasts, whether that comes through employment, entrepreneurship, freelancing, digital work or another form of economic participation.

Research and verification

We are looking for Zimbabweans willing to share their experience so we can build a solution that starts from the ground up. Help us understand the status quo better by filling in this form.
